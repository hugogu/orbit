# Trademark and official distribution policy

“ORBIT”, “ORBIT Solar System Observatory”, the ORBIT logo, and related branding identify the official project and services of the maintainer.

The PolyForm Noncommercial license does not grant a trademark license. Forks and modified deployments may use the source code under the applicable license, but must choose a different product name, logo, and domain unless the maintainer gives written permission.

Only releases, container images, and hosted services published or explicitly authorized by the maintainer may describe themselves as **Official ORBIT**. This policy does not restrict factual statements such as “a fork of ORBIT” that do not imply endorsement.
